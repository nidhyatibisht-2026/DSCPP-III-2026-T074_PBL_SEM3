JOIN THE NETWORK

Register

Register once to get your ID. You need this ID to donate food (Donors) or to place requests (NGOs & Animal Shelters).

Why register?

  1. Get your unique ID The backend assigns an ID used on every donation or request.
  2. One phone, one account 10-digit phones are validated and kept unique per entity.
  3. Stay accountable Penalties for spoiled food are tracked per registered entity.
🌱 Every registered member helps reduce food waste.

NEW DONATION

Donate surplus food

Fill in the food details — the matching engine pairs your surplus with waiting requests automatically.

📍 Pickup address is taken from the address you registered with.

How donations work

  1. List surplus food Servings, shelf-life and diet type set its urgency on the radar.
  2. Auto-matching Ingredients are screened for allergens; NGOs and shelters get only compatible, safe food.
  3. Pickup & handshake Orders appear under Pickups — both sides confirm delivery.
🌱 Every donation helps reduce food waste.
